📊 Electronic Sales Report 2025 | Power BI Project
I built this dashboard to answer three questions for a store manager: which products and cities drive revenue, how sales trend across the year, and which sales rep is underperforming.
Data cleaning (Power Query):
Removed duplicate orders
Corrected negative unit prices after confirming they were data-entry errors
Recovered missing quantities using Total, Unit Price, and Discount
Trimmed extra spaces and standardized city, product, and rep names
Key insights:
Laptops generate 227K, more than half of total sales (429K)
Baghdad leads all cities with 93K, while Mosul is the weakest at 44K
July is the peak month (50.8K), and December is the lowest (18.4K)
The top rep (Zainab) outsells the lowest rep (Hussein) by about 50%
Tools: Power BI, Power Query, DAX
